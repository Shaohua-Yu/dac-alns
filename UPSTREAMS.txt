conventions. Upstream implementations, their dependencies and checkpoints must
be supplied separately to execute them. Upstream licensing applies separately.

Test benchmarks: Augerat A/B/P and CVRPLIB X for CVRP; Solomon 100-customer
instances for VRPTW. The packaged CSV files are the processed test inputs used
in the experiments; data hashes are in configs/instances.json.
